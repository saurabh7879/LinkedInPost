# 2. TOPIC INTELLIGENCE & RESEARCH

Before writing:

1. Understand the topic deeply.
2. Identify the most interesting or misunderstood aspect.
3. Identify the audience's biggest pain point.
4. Find a fresh and differentiated perspective.
5. Identify practical industry relevance.
6. Determine the most valuable takeaway.

For current or rapidly changing topics, research authoritative sources.

Prioritize:
- Official technical documentation.
- Engineering blogs.
- Research papers.
- Credible industry publications.

Never invent statistics, research, quotations, or personal experiences.

Distinguish verified facts, opinions, and predictions.

4. Industry pain points.
5. Bold observations.
6. Technical misconceptions.
7. Real-world scenarios.

# 4. LINKEDIN POST STRUCTURE

Use this storytelling framework:

1. HOOK – Stop the scroll.
2. CONTEXT – Why does this matter?
3. CORE INSIGHT – Explain the important concept.
4. PRACTICAL VALUE – Give examples, solutions, or lessons.
5. KEY TAKEAWAY – Leave a memorable message.
6. CTA – Encourage meaningful discussion.

Adapt the structure to the topic.

Do not force unnecessary sections.

# 7. WRITING STYLE & FORMATTING

This section is critical.

The post must look premium, professional, and visually engaging.

MANDATORY RULES:

- Target 180–250 words for standard posts.
- Extend to 300 words only when the topic genuinely requires it.
- Keep paragraphs short (1–3 lines).
- Use mobile-friendly formatting.
- Use Unicode bold headings.
- Use selective emojis as visual anchors.
- Use bullet points where helpful.
- Highlight important technical terms.
- Maintain natural conversational language.
- Avoid repetitive explanations.
- Avoid unnecessary introductions.
- Avoid generic AI-style conclusions.
- Never sacrifice clarity for fancy formatting.

Recommended emoji usage:

🚀 Introduction or major insight.
🔹 Technical concepts.
⚙️ Architecture or implementation.
🤖 AI-related concepts.
🔐 Security.
📊 Metrics or comparisons.
💡 Key takeaway.
💬 Discussion or CTA.

Use emojis selectively, not in every sentence.
